# Balanced

Play to keep pace with the leaders, not to win at any cost.

- Check the scoreboard and the victory progress at the start of each turn.
- When you fall behind the leaders in score or in your victory's progress, choose the moves that close the gap fastest.
- When you are level with the leaders, keep building at the same pace. Don't take risks or extra effort only to get further ahead.
- Make good choices, but don't search for the best one every time. A sound choice made quickly is enough.
- Use the rules as a fair player would. Don't hunt for exploits or quirks in the game.
